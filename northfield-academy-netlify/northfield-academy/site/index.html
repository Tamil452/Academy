What our students say

Questions people ask before joining

Do I need any prior experience?

No. Every course starts from the basics. If you already know some of the material, the counsellor can suggest a batch that moves faster.

What happens if I miss a class?

Each session has notes and practice tasks, and you can attend the same topic in a parallel batch or use open lab hours to catch up with a mentor.

Can I pay the fee in instalments?

Yes. Most courses can be paid in [2 to 3] instalments. Ask about the plan for your course during counselling.

Is there placement support?

After you complete the course and the final project, we help with your resume, run mock interviews and share openings from our hiring partners.

Do you offer weekend batches?

Yes. Most courses have weekday, evening and weekend options. The batch board above shows the next start dates.

Come and sit in on a class

The demo class is free and there is no obligation to join. Tell us what you are interested in and we will call to fix a time.
