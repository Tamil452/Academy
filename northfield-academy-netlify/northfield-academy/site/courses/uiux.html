What you will be able to do

  • Plan and run user interviews
  • Map user journeys and information architecture
  • Wireframe and prototype in Figma
  • Apply typography, colour and layout principles
  • Build and use a design system
  • Write a portfolio case study

Who this course is for

  • Students and graduates interested in design
  • Graphic designers moving into digital products
  • Developers who want to design better interfaces

Before you start

No design experience needed. A sense of curiosity about how things work helps.

Syllabus

1Design thinking and researchWeeks 1–3
  • The design process
  • User interviews
  • Personas
  • Problem statements
2Structure and flowsWeeks 4–5
  • Information architecture
  • User flows
  • Sketching
  • Wireframes
3Visual designWeeks 6–8
  • Typography
  • Colour
  • Layout grids
  • Components
4Prototyping and testingWeeks 9–10
  • Figma prototyping
  • Usability testing
  • Iterating on feedback
5PortfolioWeeks 11–12
  • Case study writing
  • Portfolio review
  • Presentation

What you will build

Each project is reviewed by your instructor and goes into your portfolio.

App redesign

Research and redesign a part of an app people complain about, backed by user tests.

Design system

A small component library with colours, type and reusable components in Figma.

Case study

A complete project from research to prototype, written up for your portfolio.

Tools you will use

  • Figma
  • FigJam
  • Maze
  • Notion
