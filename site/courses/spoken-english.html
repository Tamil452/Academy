What you will be able to do

  • Speak clearly and confidently in everyday situations
  • Introduce yourself well in interviews
  • Take part in group discussions
  • Give short presentations
  • Write clear professional emails
  • Answer common interview questions

Who this course is for

  • Students preparing for placements
  • Job seekers
  • Professionals who want to speak up more at work

Before you start

You should be able to read simple English. We place you in a group that matches your level.

Syllabus

1Everyday conversationWeeks 1–2
  • Pronunciation
  • Common phrases
  • Small talk
2Speaking at workWeeks 3–4
  • Meetings
  • Presentations
  • Professional email
3InterviewsWeeks 5–6
  • Self-introduction
  • Group discussion
  • Recorded mock interviews

What you will build

Each project is reviewed by your instructor and goes into your portfolio.

Self-introduction video

A recorded introduction you refine with feedback until it is interview-ready.

Group discussion

A graded discussion on a current topic with peer and trainer feedback.

Mock interview

A full recorded mock interview reviewed with you line by line.

Tools you will use

  • Recorded practice sessions
  • Vocabulary workbook
  • Mock interview kit
