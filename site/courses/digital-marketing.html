What you will be able to do

  • Plan a digital marketing strategy
  • Improve a website’s search ranking
  • Run Google and Meta ad campaigns
  • Create content for social media
  • Set up email marketing
  • Measure results with Google Analytics

Who this course is for

  • Graduates looking for a marketing role
  • Small business owners
  • Freelancers who want to offer marketing services

Before you start

No prior experience needed. You should be comfortable using social media and the web.

Syllabus

1FoundationsWeeks 1–2
  • Marketing funnels
  • Audience research
  • Websites and landing pages
2SearchWeeks 3–5
  • Keyword research
  • On-page SEO
  • Google Ads
3Social mediaWeeks 6–8
  • Content planning
  • Instagram and LinkedIn
  • Meta Ads
4Email and analyticsWeeks 9–10
  • Email campaigns
  • Google Analytics 4
  • Reporting
5Live campaignWeeks 11–12
  • Plan
  • Run with a real budget
  • Report results

What you will build

Each project is reviewed by your instructor and goes into your portfolio.

SEO audit

A full audit of a real local business website with a prioritised fix list.

Ad campaign

A Google or Meta campaign you plan, run and optimise with a small budget.

Marketing plan

A three-month plan for a business, presented to the class.

Tools you will use

  • Google Ads
  • Meta Ads Manager
  • Google Analytics 4
  • Search Console
  • Canva
  • Mailchimp
